# RESQ – Prompt Engineering & Development Journey

## Project Name

**RESQ – AI Smart Emergency Response & Ambulance Coordination System**

## Purpose of this Document

This document highlights the major prompts we used during development of RESQ.

Instead of randomly generating code, we used structured prompts to build different modules of the project.

This document summarizes only the **10 most important prompts** used in development.
